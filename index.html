    Thin-file credit underwriting and risk scoring engine for the Indian financial ecosystem. Processes statement data via the RBI Account Aggregator framework with a dual-model XGBoost ensemble and SHAP explainability, deployed via Dockerized FastAPI.

    Fundamental analysis and stock screening engine for Indian equities (NSE). Evaluates stocks using an 11-criteria quality score and margin-of-safety valuation, featuring autonomous AI research agents for in-depth company synthesis.

    Agricultural diagnosis platform powered by a Dual-Head Vision Transformer (ViT), classifying across 55 crop types and 175 disease types simultaneously from leaf photos. Delivers treatment plans and automated PDF reports via FastAPI.
